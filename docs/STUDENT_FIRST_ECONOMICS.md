# Stuora Student-First Economics

## Product rule

Stuora is built to reduce the cost of student life, not add another tax to it.

Core participation should remain free for students wherever practical. Revenue should come primarily from organizations that benefit from access to student demand: employers, verified housing providers, local businesses, travel partners, service partners, sponsorships, and optional premium commercial tools.

## Student protections

1. **Core account access is free.** Students can create a profile, browse, search, message, join communities, and use the core discovery surfaces without a mandatory subscription.
2. **Jobs are free for applicants.** Stuora never charges a student merely to apply for a job.
3. **Ordinary peer-to-peer listings are free.** A student can sell an ordinary personal item without a listing fee. Paid boosts may exist later, but must remain optional.
4. **Housing search has no student application fee from Stuora.** Professional housing providers may pay for commercial tools or visibility; any third-party charge shown to students must be disclosed before action.
5. **Trips prioritize student pricing.** Stuora should seek group rates, partner discounts, and sponsored offers, but must not pretend a discount exists when it does not.
6. **Events can be free or discounted.** Sponsored and partner-funded student pricing should be visibly labeled.
7. **Services can use student-to-student rates.** Stuora may facilitate tutoring, creative work, moving help, and other lawful services while making all fees transparent.
8. **No hidden fees.** Any transaction, protection, booking, or payment fee must be shown before confirmation.

## Subsidy integrity

Every offer labeled **Subsidized**, **Sponsored**, **Student price**, **Discount**, or **Cashback** must store enough information to explain the claim:

- who funds or provides the benefit;
- the normal/original price where applicable;
- the student price or benefit value;
- the validity period;
- eligibility rules;
- verification status;
- terms/source reference.

A client app must not be allowed to mark its own offer as verified. Verified benefits are created or approved through privileged server/admin workflows.

## Sustainable revenue model

Stuora should preferentially monetize the demand side rather than the student side:

- employer job postings and recruiting tools;
- professional housing-provider subscriptions;
- verified local-business promotions;
- sponsored student benefits;
- optional marketplace boosts;
- event organizer tools and ticketing fees;
- travel/referral partnerships;
- optional transaction-protection fees;
- premium business analytics;
- grants, city/student-welfare partnerships, and sponsorship programmes.

## Product metric

In addition to growth and revenue, Stuora should measure **student value created**:

- money saved through verified benefits;
- free items redistributed;
- affordable rooms discovered;
- paid work opportunities accessed;
- trip/event discounts unlocked;
- fees avoided compared with alternatives.

The product should be able to report a credible cumulative `student_savings_value` rather than merely claiming to be student-friendly.
